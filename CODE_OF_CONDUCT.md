# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, or sexualized language or attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the
project, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and community forums.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bwente). Reports will be reviewed promptly,
handled as confidentially as practical, and evaluated fairly. Project maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy.
